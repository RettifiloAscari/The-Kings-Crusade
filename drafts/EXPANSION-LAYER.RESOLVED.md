# Design Draft: The Expansion Layer

Bringing the set from a proven skeleton (63 pages) toward the scale and feature
set of a published 5e campaign setting. Four gated decisions were put to the DM as
multiple choice and all four came back on the recommended option; this draft records
what those answers commit us to, what is natural extension of approved canon, and
what remains genuinely open.

**Approved by the DM, this pass:**

1. **Faith — "Split faiths."** An institutional pantheon across the mundane realms;
   Elduvaine the exception. The schism is a political fault line inside the coalition.
2. **Geography — "Full gazetteer."** Both realms and everything between: regions,
   keyed settlements, travel times, regional encounter tables.
3. **Player crunch — "Full chapter."** Backgrounds, feats, subclasses, spells, items.
4. **Royals — "Name them all."** House Ysolde named and peopled, including the
   leper-king-model figure; Oksitan's king named and drowned.

---

## 1. The Nine Works, and why Elduvaine has no church

**The theology is derived from canon rather than bolted onto it.** Harrowmark's land
does nothing. Its people therefore built everything themselves, and the religion that
grew there is a religion of *the made thing*: the Concord teaches that the gods
**worked and then withdrew**, leaving a world deliberately unfinished so that hands
would have something to do. They are not called gods. They are called **the Works**.

| The Work | Sphere | Domains (2014 PHB) |
|---|---|---|
| Ashet the Anvil | Craft, making, the honest tool | Knowledge, War |
| Voran of the Long Road | Travel, messengers, guest-right | Life, Trickery |
| Sennet the Witness | Oath, contract, law, testimony | Knowledge, Trickery |
| Halevin the Hearth-Kept | Home, harvest, healing | Life, Nature |
| Aurine the Unshuttered | Light, truth, courage, dawn | Light |
| Duran Ninefold | War as discipline, the held line | War |
| Threnn Greywater | Sea, storm, river, the drowned | Tempest |
| Ossuar the Quiet Warden | Death, the grave, remembrance | Death |
| Saveth of the Green Verge | Wilds, beasts, the turning seasons | Nature |

**Elduvaine never built this.** You do not raise a temple to ask for an answer in a
country where the river answers directly. Elduvaine has **Observances** instead —
local, seasonal, addressed to the habits rather than to persons — and its religious
professionals are **Keepers**, who tend a habit the way a Concord priest tends a
congregation. That the wizard's own title was Keeper of the Ysolde Archive is now
considerably darker, and is intended to be.

### The Tenth Work — the fault line

A militant order within the Concord holds that Elduvaine's habits are not heresy but
**an unfinished divine work** — the tenth and last, left for hands to complete. They
march with the crusade to complete it. They are pious, disciplined, personally brave,
and entirely prepared to consecrate a Kept Season orchard by burning it.

This is the Reynald de Chatillon slot the *Kingdom of Heaven* touchstone reserves:
complexity among the crusaders, not in the cause. It also puts real pressure on the
Promise, since a share in Elduvaine's resident magic means one thing to a lord who
wants an estate and quite another to an order that wants the habits sanctified.

**Deliberately not answered:** whether the Works exist. Divine magic functions; that
is all the campaign asserts. Elduvish clerics cast from the Observances and Concord
clerics from the Works, and neither side's spells fail.

## 2. House Ysolde

An elven house, mixed by two centuries of marriage. Canon already fixed three members
by title and one attribute each; this names them and adds two.

| Name | Race | Style | Status | What they want after the war |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maelis Ysolde | Elf | The Veiled Sovereign | Held in Caer Ysolde | The habits restored, whatever it costs her |
| Aveline Ysolde | Human | The Regent | **At large** | Elduvaine free, and the Archive closed forever |
| Ninian Ysolde | Half-elf | The Ward | Freed in Module 4 | The coalition paid, or Elduvaine buys a second war |
| Ottoline Vahn | Gnome | The Magistrate | Freed in Module 8 | The institutions rebuilt first, everything else after |
| Emrys Ysolde | Elf | The Envoy | Held, third site | Whatever keeps the most people alive |

**Maelis is the leper king.** She has been dying for eleven years of a wasting that
came out of the land itself: a sovereign of Elduvaine is bound to the habits, and the
habits are being drained. **She is dying because Elduvaine is.** Veiled, immobile,
and the cleverest person in the campaign. Her decline is a second clock running
alongside Norvatch's ledgers, and unlike the ledgers it cannot be bought.

**Emrys has been talking to Vale.** Not treachery — he is the only one who will, and
three years of it have kept his sister alive and a great many hostages unharmed. He
has also told Vale things. He does not defend himself and will not apologise. This is
complexity in the costs, not in the cause; Vale remains a villain throughout.

United on the war, divided on what follows it, exactly as canon required. **The
campaign still does not adjudicate that argument** — five positions, no verdict.

## 3. Oksitan, and the king the river took

**King Raimon V of Oksitan**, human, seventy-one, grey, and entirely undeterred by
his years — the Barbarossa note the Middle High German lay supplies. He takes the road
the party does not, and drowns fording the **Vaskren** at the head of his own column.
His army comes apart behind him exactly as canon specified.

**What Oksitan wants — now decided.** Not land and not the Archive: **the Willing
Road.** Oksitan is ford country, and its whole political economy is the control of
movement. A road that shortens for travellers who mean well is, to an Oksitan lord,
the most valuable object in the world, and its houses intend to claim the waystones
and learn to cut more.

They cannot. **Nobody has ever established what the Willing Road measures**, and the
campaign is on record that it never will — so Oksitan is chasing a thing it is
structurally guaranteed never to have. Raimon goes in person at seventy-one because he
wants to walk that road once before he dies, and a river kills him on the way.

**This does not close the Deliberately Open question.** It gives it a price.

**Still open and NOT decided here: what Auberitz wants.** It was not among the four
questions put to the DM and is not invented in this pass. Auberitz's *role* — the
siege train, the engineers, the quartermasters — is canon and is built out; its
agenda stays on the open list.

## 4. The Gazetteer

Six regional chapters, roughly thirty keyed entries, every one hung off geography
already established rather than replacing it.

- **Harrowmark** — Duncarrow (existing), Greywatch (existing), plus Ellendrake the
  fleet port, Stannock's crag armouries, Corrieholt in the orc high country,
  Fenmarrow's thin farm country, Kettleburn.
- **The Sea Road** — Calanthe (existing) and the wrecking coast around it.
- **The Mountain Road** — Ashgate Ford (existing), the Cold Stair, the Old Workings
  (existing), Kir Halloway, Barrowfell.
- **Elduvaine: the Braid** — Caer Ysolde (existing), Sennoch Hall (existing),
  Lisswater, the Threefold Stair.
- **Elduvaine: the Vaunt** — Vindana (existing), Caerwyn (existing), the Dead Mile
  (existing), Morgarth.
- **Elduvaine: the Orchard Marches and the Standing Marches** — Bryn Aeling (the
  Held Winter of Module 5), Nantcorrow, Cairn Ithel's light-stone quarries, Ysgaron.
- **The coalition realms**, in brief: Oksitan, Auberitz, Norvatch.

Every settlement entry carries population, who holds it, what the occupation did to
it where applicable, and at least one hook. Regional encounter tables per chapter.

## 5. What else the set was missing

Audited against published 5e setting books and adventure paths:

- **Timeline** — nine centuries of Elduvaine compressed, then the three years in detail.
- **Factions with ranks** — six, with renown-style advancement.
- **Languages, calendar, names** — including the joke that Elduvaine cannot agree on
  a calendar because its woods keep different seasons.
- **Downtime** — activities that mean something on a march.
- **Travel and hazards** — the Willing Road as a mechanic, weather, supply.
- **A bestiary** — resident-magic creatures, the Sixth Free Legion's ranks, and stat
  blocks for the named NPCs.
- **Character options** — backgrounds, feats, subclasses, spells, magic items.
- **In the modules: puzzles.** There were none. This is the largest single gap
  against any published module and the DM called it out by name.
- **In the modules: keyed locations, full-mechanics traps, set pieces, regional
  encounter tables, handouts, sidebars.**
